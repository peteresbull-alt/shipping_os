Our Story

Moving the world's goods,one shipment at a time.

Fracht Express was built on a simple idea: freight forwarding should be fast, transparent, and never leave you guessing where your cargo is.

From Frustration to Fracht Express

We got tired of freight forwarders that hide the ball.

Fracht Express started with a straightforward frustration: booking a shipment shouldn't require five phone calls, and knowing where your cargo is shouldn't require a favor from a warehouse manager.

So we built a platform that puts the entire shipping lifecycle — quoting, booking, payment, and live tracking — into one dashboard. No black boxes, no surprise fees, no "let me check and call you back."

Today we move freight by air, sea and road across 150+ countries, backed by a network of regional hubs and an in-house customs desk that keeps cargo moving instead of sitting in a queue.
