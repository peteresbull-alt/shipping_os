Here's exactly what happens to your consignment from the moment it's booked with us to the moment it arrives.

Every Last Mile

From our hands to the doorstep, every step is tracked.

01

Your consignment is booked

Our team creates a record for your shipment — package details, sender and recipient — and issues a unique tracking number tied to it.

02

It's picked up

A courier collects the consignment from the sender and logs it into our network — the first checkpoint on its journey.

03

It moves through our network

Your goods travel by air, sea or road through our regional hubs. Every checkpoint — departure, customs, arrival — posts a live update the moment it happens.

04

You track it, anytime

Enter your tracking number on our site to see the current status and full location history — no account, no login, no waiting on hold.
